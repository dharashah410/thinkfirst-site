Privacy

ThinkFirst keeps your family’s details on your own computer. A few things leave it, and this page names each one.

What stays on your computer

These are saved in ThinkFirst’s own folder on your computer. We never receive them.

  • Your child’s nickname, age and the names or schools you asked the app to keep private.
  • The rules you set for each situation, your co-sign, and your reviews.
  • The short notes your child writes after using the chat, and the pauses the app recorded (a pause says which kind of topic came up, not the words).
  • Your PIN, saved in a scrambled form, so it cannot be read back.
  • Saved chats, only if you turn saving on. It is off by default. If you turn it on, your child is told you can read their chats. You can delete them at any time.

What is sent to ThinkFirst

ThinkFirst is free. In return we ask for a little information, kept on Supabase, a hosted database service.

  • Your email address, once you ask for a sign-in code. We use it to send you the code, tell you about updates, and learn how many families use the app. The code is sent through Resend, an email delivery service, so your address passes through it.
  • Once a day, when you open the app: a random number made on your computer (it is not made from your name or email and is not linked to your email), the date, the app version, and whether your computer is a Mac or Windows PC. This counts how many installs are in use.
  • On the download page: which version you clicked, so we can count downloads.

What is never sent to ThinkFirst

Your child’s name, age, rules, notes, pauses, or anything they type. The app has no advertising and no tracking tools.

What is sent to OpenAI

The chat is answered by Codex, which is OpenAI’s product, using your own ChatGPT account. The chat is off until you agree to this in Settings with your PIN.

When the chat is on, what your child types goes to OpenAI together with your rules for that situation and the earlier messages of that chat. Their reply comes back to the app. This uses your account and counts against your Codex plan. OpenAI’s own terms and privacy policy apply to it, and they set age rules. Please read them before you turn the chat on.

Before anything is sent, the app checks the message for the stop topics and for the names you asked it to keep private. A message that matches is not sent. The app asks Codex not to keep a record of the chat on your computer, and it blocks Codex from using any tools, files or the internet.

Your choices

  • Settings > Delete all data removes your cards, notes, saved chats and PIN from this computer. It cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy.
  • Settings > Export all data gives you a file of what the app holds.
  • To sign out of OpenAI for ThinkFirst, remove the chat connection in Settings, or sign out of your ChatGPT account.
  • To have your email removed from ThinkFirst, write to us. Deleting data in the app does not remove it. The daily counts hold no email and are not linked to one.

Good to know

  • The research notes in the app come from the Compass Collaborative. Reading them sends nothing anywhere.
  • The download page is on an ordinary web host, which may keep the usual server records of visits.
  • ThinkFirst is a family rule that the app enforces. It is not a guarantee of safety. If something worries you, the app has a help page, and you can always talk to your child.
  • If this page changes in a way that matters, the new version will be in the app and on the download page, with a new date.
