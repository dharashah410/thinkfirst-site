Decide how AI fits into your child's life.

AI can help children learn, explore and create. But not everything needs AI, and not every child is ready for it. ThinkFirst helps families decide when, why and how children use AI, and sets boundaries that put their own thinking first.

AI doesn't need to be part of everything.

AI may be helpful in some situations, but children also need space to think, try, make mistakes and figure things out on their own. ThinkFirst helps you decide where AI belongs, where it doesn't, and what your child should be able to do independently.

Homework and schoolworkLearning a concept or finishing an assignment.
Creative projectsStories, drawings, songs, builds.
Finding out informationQuestions about the world, projects, things they are curious about.

Set the boundaries together.

About ten minutes to set up. You do it with your child, so the rules are theirs too.

  1. Choose a situation

    Start with homework, creative projects or exploring something they're curious about.

  2. Decide what AI can and cannot do

    Choose what your child can do independently, when they need your permission, and when they should stop and talk to you. Make the rules together, in words you both understand.

  3. Let them explore within those boundaries

    Your child can use ThinkFirst's chat to explore ideas, ask questions and learn. The app checks their requests against the family rules, rather than leaving the boundaries to the AI alone.

AI that helps them think, not think for them.

ThinkFirst encourages children to start with their own ideas, ask questions, explore and work things out. Instead of simply giving answers, it helps them reason, learn and discover.

Guidance by age

AI needs to play a different role at different stages of childhood. ThinkFirst offers age-based guidance to help parents make informed decisions, even before their child is ready to use AI.

You decide the boundaries

You set the rules. Changing a boundary to allow more AI use requires your PIN.

No scores, no labels

ThinkFirst doesn't rate or judge your child. If a conversation raises a concern, it gives you context to decide whether you need to talk.

Chats stay private

Chats aren't saved by default. If you choose to turn saving on, your child knows you can read them.

Help when you need it

When something worries your child, they can find guidance on what to do and how to talk to you about it.

Opening it for the first time

Your computer will warn you the first time. The app is not signed with a paid developer certificate, so macOS and Windows ask you to confirm. It is a one-time step.

On a Mac

  1. Unzip the download (double-click the zip). Drag the app into your Applications folder, or leave it where it is.
  2. Double-click the app. A message says it cannot be checked for malicious software. Click Done (not “Move to Trash”).
  3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the message about the app and click Open Anyway. Enter your Mac password.
  4. On an older Mac: right-click the app, choose Open, then click Open in the box.

On Windows

  1. Unzip the download (right-click it, then Extract All).
  2. Open the folder and double-click the installer or the app.
  3. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

Frequently asked questions

Does ThinkFirst make AI safe for children?

No app can guarantee that AI is safe. ThinkFirst helps your family set boundaries and applies them within its own chat. It is one part of how you guide your child, not a replacement for your judgement or conversations with them.

Does it cost anything?

ThinkFirst is free. AI chat uses your own Codex allowance, which is part of your eligible ChatGPT plan.

Can I read my child’s chats?

Chats are not saved by default. If you enable chat saving, your child is informed that you can read their conversations.

Why does it ask for my email?

So we can tell you about updates and learn how many families use it. You enter it once and confirm it with a code. We never receive your child’s details.

Can I use it without the internet?

The first sign-in code needs the internet. After that you can set up cards and read the guidance offline. The chat needs the internet, because the replies come from OpenAI through your account.

Can I change the rules later?

Yes. You can review and tighten rules at any time. Loosening rules requires your PIN.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete all your data from Settings within the app, then uninstall ThinkFirst from your computer.
